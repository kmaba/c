About Grand Ballroom Events

Grand Ballroom Events is a premium event venue located in Perth, Western Australia. We offer elegant spaces for weddings, birthday celebrations, corporate functions, school events, cultural celebrations and private parties — all supported by a full range of services including event space hire, catering packages, decoration options, entertainment arrangements and event planning support.

Whether you are planning an intimate dinner or a grand gala, our dedicated events team works with you from the first enquiry to the final dance, so you can focus on celebrating while we handle every detail.

Catering Packages

Flexible menus crafted by our in-house chefs to suit every occasion and dietary requirement.

Decoration Options

Custom themes, floral arrangements and table styling tailored to your vision.

Entertainment

Stage, sound and lighting hire with recommendations for DJs, bands and MCs.

Event Planning

End-to-end coordination, from vendor management to a flawless run sheet on the day.

Ballroom Facilities

Our venue is designed to impress at every scale, from an intimate celebration to a full-capacity gala of 500 guests.

The Main Ballroom

Spacious, pillar-free floor with dazzling chandeliers and a capacity of up to 500 guests.

Stage & Lighting

Professional stage, ambient lighting rigs and a high-quality sound system for every performance.

VIP Lounge

A private lounge for bridal parties, VIP guests and pre-event receptions.

Catering Kitchen

Fully equipped commercial kitchen so our chefs can serve fresh food made to order.

Bridal Suite

A stylish private suite with dressing tables and mirror lighting for getting ready in comfort.

Parking & Access

Ample on-site parking, wheelchair-accessible entry and convenient access for guests of all ages.
